AI-Driven Public Health Chatbot for Disease Awareness

Problem Addressed: Rural and semi-urban communities often face barriers in accessing reliable health awareness, verified disease prevention information, and timely vaccination schedules due to language differences and limited internet access.

Developed a team-based multilingual conversational AI chatbot engineered to democratize access to health awareness. Built with Dialogflow NLU and a custom Flask backend, the system delivers verified symptom awareness, prevention guidance, disease information, and vaccination alerts in multiple regional languages.

Key Features & Contributions:

  • Authoritative Knowledge Extraction: Sourced verified medical guidance and health information from the World Health Organization (WHO) using automated web scraping with BeautifulSoup.
  • Multilingual Translation: Integrated MyMemory Translation API to enable dynamic conversational interaction in vernacular languages.
  • Omnichannel Reach: Connected the conversational pipeline to Twilio API to support direct engagement over WhatsApp and SMS without requiring heavy app downloads.
  • Cloud Deployment & Hackathon Recognition: Deployed the Flask backend onto Render; pitched and demonstrated as a collaborative team of 6 at the Smart India Hackathon, achieving 16th rank in the internal round.
